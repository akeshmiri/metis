# 1 · Choose the opportunity strategy

## Confirm this is an opportunity

An opportunity is an uncertain event with a **positive** effect on an objective.
Not a benefit already committed to — that is a plan, and it belongs in the plan.
If it will happen unless something goes wrong, the risk is the going-wrong, and
that is a threat.

## Choose against value and controllability

| Situation | Strategy |
|---|---|
| High value, and within the team's power to guarantee | **Exploit** — remove the uncertainty and make it certain |
| Real but not certain, and worth effort | **Enhance** — raise the probability, the impact, or both |
| Capturing it needs capability the team does not have | **Share** — a partner takes part of the benefit in exchange for making it reachable |
| Low value, or pursuit costs more than the gain | **Accept** — take it if it arrives, pursue nothing |
| Beyond this project's remit to pursue | **Escalate** — hand it to whoever can act |

## The two that get chosen wrongly

**Exploit is not optimism.** It means committing effort to *make certain* — and
that effort is reassigned from something else. If nothing was reassigned, the
strategy was Enhance or Accept, whatever the register says.

**Share is not giving it away.** The partner takes a share of the benefit because
they supply the capability to capture it. Recording it as a loss misses why it
was chosen and makes it look like a bad deal in review.

## Record the strategy with the reason

Same rule as for threats: the word alone is not a decision. Write why this one.

## Then plan it

Hand to `metis-risk-manager-response-planning`. An opportunity with no owner and
no trigger is one that will be noticed after it has passed.
