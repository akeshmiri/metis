# 01 — gather, and record what would not come

## Actions

1. Read coverage, validation, reconciliation and the component version — in one
   pass. Two reads are two chances for the graph to move under one figure.
2. For each one that did not come back, record the cause. Do not retry silently
   and do not substitute a default.
3. Note the provenance mix of the criteria. All `code_derived` is a real and
   reportable state, not a gap to apologise for.

## Forbidden substitutions

- Do not compute a percentage over a denominator you did not verify.
- Do not treat a missing `Component` as version zero. It is "not recorded".

## Drift check

Every figure gathered should belong to the scope asked for. A figure from a
neighbouring journey is worse than a missing one.

## Report

Each figure with its state: measured, unmeasured with a cause, or unavailable.
