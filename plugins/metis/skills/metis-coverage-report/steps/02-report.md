# 02 — report

## Actions

1. Lead with what the numbers mean: coverage, not outcome.
2. Give the figures with their version and commit.
3. Give `unmeasured` in full, each with its cause, before any summary.
4. State what caps confidence, in one sentence.
5. If the user asked "is it ready to release", answer the question they can have
   — what is covered, what is not, what could not be measured — and say plainly
   that the readiness judgement is theirs and why this system will not make it.

## Forbidden substitutions

- Do not round an unmeasured figure to zero to make a table tidy.
- Do not add a traffic light, a grade, or a percentage-of-percentages.

## Drift check

Re-read what was asked. A report that answers a different, easier question is
the failure this step exists to prevent.

## Report

The figures, the absences, the cap — and no verdict.
