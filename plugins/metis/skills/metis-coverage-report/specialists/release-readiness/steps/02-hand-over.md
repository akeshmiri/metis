# 02 — hand the decision over

## Actions

1. Lead with what the numbers mean: coverage, not outcome.
2. Give the unmeasured list in full, before any summary.
3. State the cap on confidence in one sentence.
4. Say explicitly that the readiness judgement is the reader's, and why this
   system will not make it.

## Forbidden substitutions

- Do not answer "are we ready?" with a number that will be read as yes or no.
- Do not soften a structural gap into an operational one, or the reverse.

## Drift check

Re-read what was asked. A report answering an easier question is the failure
this step exists to prevent.

## Report

The evidence, the absences, the cap — and no verdict.
